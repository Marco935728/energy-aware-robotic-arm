# Energy-Aware Robotic Arm Control
**Research Portfolio**

> **Goal:** Can an AI learn to mirror human hand movements using minimum energy—and can I prove it using the First Principles of Physics?

## 🎯 The Mission
In prosthetics and space robotics, battery life is the ultimate constraint. This project explores the intersection of **Classical Mechanics** and **Reinforcement Learning** to optimize trajectories for efficiency, not just accuracy.

## 🏗️ Technical Stack
- **Physics Engine:** PyBullet (Rigid Body Dynamics)
- **Language:** Python
- **Math Focus:** Rotational Dynamics, Forward/Inverse Kinematics, Numerical Integration.
